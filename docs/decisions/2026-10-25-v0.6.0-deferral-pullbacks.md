# Bugs 44, 56 and 51 are fixed before v0.6.0; the other deferrals ship as known issues (2026-10-25)

**Decided by:** Philip, 2026-10-25 · **Status:** Decided
**Led to:** PLAN.md Next Up, "v0.6.0 cut set" group (Bugs 44, 56, 51); PR 87's other
deferrals stay in `BUG.md`

## What prompted it
PR 87 set the version to 0.6.0 and also re-sorted Next Up under the 2026-09-22 rule. That
sort moved 30 bugs, plus Bug 65, out of Next Up and marked them `Deferred: after v0.6.0`.
A side review pointed out that these deferrals rode inside what looked like a version
bump. Philip was shown the full list, grouped, and asked which to pull back before the
release.

## What we weighed
- **Pull back 44, 56 and 51** — two security holes in shipped behavior (`process_run`
  runs shell metacharacters found in its arguments; `klar run` writes its binary to a
  guessable `/tmp` path) and one build that reports success after a parse error. Chosen.
- **Keep every deferral** — ship 0.6.0 sooner, list all 31 as known issues. Rejected:
  a release should not knowingly ship a command-injection path or a false success.
- **A larger custom set** — not asked for; the rest stay deferred.

## What we decided, and why
Philip chose option 1: Bugs 44, 56 and 51 go back into Next Up, inside the v0.6.0 cut
set, and are fixed before the tag. Two security holes and a failed build reported as a
success should not be in a release. The other 28 deferred bugs and Bug 65 stay deferred
and are listed as known issues in the release notes.

## What it does not settle
Whether any of the remaining deferrals should move up if the trial project hits one.
Bug 122 (`true.to_string()` prints "1") stays outside the cut set and is named in the
known-issues list.
